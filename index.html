DEATHENGINE

Modern metal ecosystem — amp, cabs, downtune, clarity, mastering

  • Modern Metal
  • Thall
  • Djent
  • Industrial
  • Argent
  • Bass

One plugin instead of a chain of eight. Gate, tightening, downtuning, amp, dual cabs, clarity control, post EQ, width and limiting — in the order you'd actually build them, on a single panel you can see all at once.

Every voice is tuned for low tunings and high gain, so the low end stays defined instead of turning to mud. Bass gets the same treatment as guitar rather than being an afterthought.

  • Input & GateThreshold, release, depth and pick control to clean up chugs before they hit the amp.
  • TightenPre-gain focus filter that kills flub on 7- and 8-strings without thinning the tone.
  • Downtune EngineShift semitones and cents with formant control and sub lock. Bypassed means zero added latency.
  • Amp VoicesIndustrial, Argent and more — each with gain, definition, presence and a four-band stack.
  • FilthExtra saturation with sag and bite, for when the voice alone isn't ugly enough.
  • Cabinet A/BTwo cabs blended live, plus room, distance, air and resonance. Load your own IRs.
  • Clarity EngineFive fixed bands at 60, 120, 500, 1.8k and 5k that hold the mix position steady as gain climbs.
  • Pre & Post PedalsSix pedals: Sub, Grind, Chainsaw, Glitch, Delay, and Reverb.
  • Post EQ & WidthFinal shaping, stereo doubler and a mono-below crossover to keep the low end centred.
Release 02
Unity package

FAST VISUAL NOVEL

A visual novel engine for people who write and draw, not people who compile

  • Unity
  • Visual Novels
  • Writers
  • Artists
  • No-code first

Most VN tools ask you to learn their language first. This one doesn't. Drop the package into a Unity project, bring your art and your script, and you have a working visual novel — without writing a line of code.

The code is still there when you want it. If you're a programmer, nothing is locked away; if you're not, nothing is in your way.

.unitypackage · Unity 2021 LTS or newer

▼Download the package Docs & source
  • Write, don't wireYour script is the source of truth. Scenes follow the words instead of the other way round.
  • Art goes in firstBuilt around backgrounds, sprites and expressions — the parts artists actually spend time on.
  • Drop-in packageImport into any Unity project. No custom editor to install, no separate runtime to learn.
  • Code when you want itEverything is open underneath, so a programmer on the team can extend it freely.
Who

One person,
two workshops.

XZLAB is owned by me, XZ (@XZAGames on YT!). I make music and I make games, and I kept running into the same problem in both: the tools either do too little or bury the thing you need under six menus or make you pay $100.

So the rule here is the same for a plugin and for a game engine — get the person to the result fast, then let them go deeper if they want to. DeathEngine puts a full metal chain on one panel. Fast Visual Novel gets a writer to a playable scene before they ever open a script file.

Everything on this page is free. If something breaks or you want a feature, open an issue on GitHub — that's the fastest way to reach me.
